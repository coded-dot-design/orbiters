# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First cut of the full game, grown from the Fizlet gallery daily Moon Rover (#123): the same round-world driving, camera and rover handling.
- Six original worlds (Pebble, Rime, Dunemar, Cinder, Prism, Halo), each with its own surface, gravity and grip, hazards, a friendly and a hostile species, and one mission (collect, race, deliver, rescue, defend, the great beacon) whose reward lights its beacon and opens the next world; a launch pad and a warp between worlds; free roam on any open world.
- Aliens you talk to with ACT: the elder (missions), the trader (crystals for credits), the outpost (gifts); hostiles on the night side ram or steal.
- Garage: pilot (suit, helmet, visor) and rover (body, wheels, paint, trim, decal; tyres, boost tank, armour, tow claw), saved on the device (versioned) with an export code.
- Site: landing page with the game as the hero, /play/.
